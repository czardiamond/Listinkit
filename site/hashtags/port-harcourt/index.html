How to use them

How to use these hashtags

  1. On Instagram, use 5–15 hashtags that genuinely match the post: one broad set, one area set, one type set.
  2. On TikTok, 3–5 tags is enough; the caption and on-screen text matter more.
  3. Rotate sets between posts so you don't paste the identical block every time.
  4. Add one branded tag of your own (for example #[YourName]Homes) so clients can find all your listings.
  5. Avoid unrelated trending tags. They bring views but not buyers.

5 to 15 on Instagram and 3 to 5 on TikTok works well for most agents.

Do hashtags still work on Instagram?

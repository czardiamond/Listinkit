PropClips alternative: ListingKit compared

PropClips is the closest tool to ListingKit: it also targets African, WhatsApp-first agents. The differences are price, currency and what you get per listing.

PropClips (propclips.live) turns a listing into captions for Instagram, Facebook, X, WhatsApp, Snapchat and TikTok, plus branded 9:16 story cards and auto video reels. It explicitly markets to agents in Nigeria, Kenya, South Africa and Egypt.

If auto-generated reels from your photos are the main thing you need, PropClips may suit you. If you want the full written kit (WhatsApp broadcast, flyer copy and a script you film yourself) priced in naira, ListingKit is built for that.

Which should you choose?

Choose PropClips if you want story cards and reels generated from photos and you're comfortable paying in dollars.

Choose ListingKit if you sell mostly on WhatsApp, need a broadcast message and flyer copy for every listing, and want to pay ₦5,000 a month instead of $39.99 or more.

They can work together: some agents will use ListingKit for the words and another tool for visuals.

How to use them

How to choose

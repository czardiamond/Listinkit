Writing for Ikoyi buyers

  1. Corporate and expatriate leases in Ikoyi are often quoted in US dollars. State the currency clearly and whether naira is accepted.
  2. Name the micro-location: Banana Island, Parkview, Osborne Foreshore and Old Ikoyi each attract a different buyer.
  3. Lead with services: power arrangement, water treatment, staff quarters, security and facility management.
  4. Keep the tone understated. Ikoyi buyers respond to specifics, not superlatives.

FAQ

Questions agents ask

What makes a shortlet description convert?

Clear photos, honest amenities (especially power and Wi-Fi), house rules, a 'from' nightly rate and a fast way to book.

Should I price Ikoyi listings in dollars or naira?

Use whatever the landlord or seller actually accepts. Many corporate leases are in dollars; if so, say so and note whether naira payment is possible.

What do Ikoyi tenants ask about most?

Power (dedicated transformer and backup generators), service charge, security, parking and how the building is managed.

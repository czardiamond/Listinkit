Writing for Magodo buyers

  1. Say which phase. Magodo GRA Phase 1 and Phase 2 (Shangisha) are distinct and buyers know the difference.
  2. Security and estate management are the main selling points; mention gates and estate rules.
  3. Plot size and compound space matter more here than in high-rise areas; give the land size.
  4. Mention road links to the Lagos-Ibadan Expressway and Ojota for commuters.

How to use them

Getting the bungalow details right

It's a gated, low-density GRA on the mainland with larger plots and quieter streets.

Should I mention the estate phase?

Yes. Name Magodo GRA Phase 1 or Phase 2 (Shangisha); buyers value them differently.

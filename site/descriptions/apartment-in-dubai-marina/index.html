Writing for Dubai Marina buyers

  1. Property ads in Dubai must carry the advertising permit number issued through the regulator's system. Add it before posting.
  2. Use square feet and state the view (full marina, partial sea, community). Views change prices sharply.
  3. Annual rents are often paid by a number of cheques; state how many the landlord accepts.
  4. Holiday homes need a permit from Dubai's tourism authority; don't advertise short stays without one.

FAQ

Questions agents ask

What should an apartment description include?

Bedrooms, floor level, lift, building amenities, power and water arrangements, parking, service charge and price or rent.

What must a Dubai Marina property ad include?

Your brokerage details and the advertising permit number required for property ads in Dubai, plus accurate size in square feet, price in AED and the view.

Can I advertise a Dubai Marina apartment as a holiday home?

Only if it holds a valid holiday-home permit from Dubai's tourism authority. Check current rules before listing.

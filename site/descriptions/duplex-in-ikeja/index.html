Writing for Ikeja buyers

  1. Name the pocket: Ikeja GRA is quiet and leafy, while Allen, Opebi and Oregun are busier and more commercial.
  2. Airport proximity is a real draw for frequent travellers and airline staff; mention it if it's genuinely close.
  3. Many Ikeja buyers work on the mainland and avoid the bridges, so frame commute times around mainland hubs.
  4. Mention power and water arrangements; mainland tenants ask early.

FAQ

Questions agents ask

What should a duplex listing description include?

Bedroom count, en suites, BQ, parking, plot size, finishing, the exact location or estate, title document and price.

Which parts of Ikeja are most sought after?

Ikeja GRA is the premium residential pocket. Allen Avenue, Opebi and Oregun suit people who want to be close to business and shopping.

Is Ikeja good for rentals?

Yes. Demand comes from mainland professionals, civil servants and people who want easy airport access.

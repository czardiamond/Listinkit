Writing for Wuse buyers

  1. Name the zone: Wuse 2 and the numbered Wuse zones are distinct, and Wuse 2 is the best known.
  2. Shortlet guests here are often in Abuja for meetings; highlight Wi-Fi, a workspace and quick routes to the Central Business District.
  3. Mention walkable conveniences such as Banex Plaza or Aminu Kano Crescent only if they're genuinely close.
  4. Parking and power are top questions for Wuse apartments.

FAQ

Questions agents ask

What should an apartment description include?

Bedrooms, floor level, lift, building amenities, power and water arrangements, parking, service charge and price or rent.

Is Wuse good for shortlets?

Yes. Wuse 2 in particular attracts business travellers and visitors who want restaurants and shops nearby.

Should I say Wuse or Wuse 2?

Be specific. Wuse 2 is a recognised, sought-after district, distinct from the other Wuse zones.

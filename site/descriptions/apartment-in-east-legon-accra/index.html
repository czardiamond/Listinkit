Writing for East Legon buyers

  1. Rents in East Legon are often quoted monthly and frequently in US dollars, with one or two years paid in advance. State the currency and advance terms.
  2. Land in Ghana is leasehold. Non-citizens can hold leases of up to 50 years; confirm the lease term and registration with a lawyer.
  3. Name the pocket: East Legon proper, East Legon Hills and Adjiringanor differ in price and access.
  4. Diaspora buyers want verified documents, a reputable developer and video tours.

FAQ

Questions agents ask

What should an apartment description include?

Bedrooms, floor level, lift, building amenities, power and water arrangements, parking, service charge and price or rent.

Should I quote East Legon rent in cedis or dollars?

Use what the landlord accepts; many East Legon rentals are quoted in US dollars. State the advance payment period too.

Can foreigners buy property in East Legon?

Non-Ghanaians can hold leasehold interests (commonly up to 50 years). Encourage buyers to confirm lease terms and Lands Commission registration with a lawyer.

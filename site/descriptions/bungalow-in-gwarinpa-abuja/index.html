Writing for Gwarinpa buyers

  1. Use the avenue or street reference; Abuja buyers navigate Gwarinpa by its numbered avenues.
  2. Abuja title documents differ from Lagos: state whether there is an FCT Certificate of Occupancy, Right of Occupancy or allocation papers.
  3. Mention access to the Kubwa Expressway and Jabi for commuters.
  4. Gwarinpa is family territory: schools, playgrounds and quiet closes sell.

FAQ

Questions agents ask

Who buys bungalows?

Older buyers, families with young children, people who want a bigger compound and buyers planning to build up later.

What title documents apply to Gwarinpa property?

Abuja property usually carries an FCT Certificate of Occupancy, Right of Occupancy or allocation papers. State exactly which, and encourage verification with AGIS through a lawyer.

How do buyers find homes in Gwarinpa?

By avenue and street. Quote the avenue (for example 3rd or 6th Avenue) so buyers can place it instantly.

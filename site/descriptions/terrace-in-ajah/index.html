Writing for Ajah buyers

  1. Ajah buyers compare value. Show what the price gets them versus closer-in Lekki.
  2. Be precise about the estate and the turn-off from the Lekki-Epe Expressway; "Ajah" covers a wide area.
  3. Drainage and access roads matter. If the estate road is tarred, say so.
  4. For land, state the exact title stage (excision, gazette, Governor's Consent) rather than "good title".

FAQ

Questions agents ask

What is the difference between a terrace and a duplex listing?

A terrace duplex shares walls with neighbouring units in a row; say how many units share the compound and what's shared.

Is Ajah good for first-time buyers?

It's popular with first-time buyers because homes and land cost less than closer-in Lekki. Present the value clearly and be honest about commute times.

What title documents are common for land around Ajah?

You'll see Governor's Consent, C of O, excision and gazette. State exactly what the land has and invite buyers to verify.

Writing for Yaba buyers

  1. Yaba renters are young and budget-aware. Lead with rent, power and internet.
  2. Proximity to UNILAG or Yaba Tech matters for student and staff housing; name the campus only if it's genuinely nearby.
  3. Fibre or good 4G coverage is a selling point for remote and tech workers.
  4. Mention the route to the Island (Third Mainland Bridge) for tenants who work in VI or Ikoyi.

FAQ

Questions agents ask

What should an apartment description include?

Bedrooms, floor level, lift, building amenities, power and water arrangements, parking, service charge and price or rent.

Who rents in Yaba?

Tech workers, young professionals, students and university staff. Lead with rent, power and internet.

Are shortlets viable in Yaba?

There is demand from visiting tech teams, students' families and event-goers. Price below Island shortlets and highlight Wi-Fi and power.

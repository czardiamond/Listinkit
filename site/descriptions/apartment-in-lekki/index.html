Writing for Lekki buyers

  1. Name the exact sub-area. "Lekki" stretches from Phase 1 to Ibeju-Lekki, and buyers price them very differently.
  2. Be ready for drainage questions: some Lekki streets flood in the rainy season, and serious buyers will ask about the specific road.
  3. Quote realistic off-peak drive times on the Lekki-Epe Expressway rather than "minutes from everywhere".
  4. Estate details sell here: the gate, security, road quality and estate dues.

FAQ

Questions agents ask

What should an apartment description include?

Bedrooms, floor level, lift, building amenities, power and water arrangements, parking, service charge and price or rent.

Which part of Lekki should I name in a listing?

Always name the sub-area or estate, such as Lekki Phase 1, Chevron, Ikota or Osapa London, rather than just "Lekki"; buyers search and price by sub-area.

What title documents do Lekki buyers expect?

Most buyers look for Governor's Consent or a C of O. For new estates, say exactly what exists, such as a registered survey or excision, and offer documents for their lawyer to verify.

Writing for Port Harcourt GRA buyers

  1. Say which phase: Old GRA and GRA Phases 1–3 are all known by name.
  2. Corporate tenants from the oil and gas sector want security, power backup and parking; lead with these.
  3. Mention access to Aba Road and Trans-Amadi for commuters.
  4. Be clear about the currency and payment terms for corporate leases.

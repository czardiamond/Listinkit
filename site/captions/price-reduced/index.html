Real estate captions

Price reduced captions for real estate

A price reduction is news. Frame it as an opportunity with a deadline, not a property nobody wanted.

Buyers who saw the listing before are your best audience for a price drop, so the caption should work for people who already know the property: lead with the new number and the saving.

Avoid words like "desperate" or "must go". "New price", "seller motivated" and "now within reach" keep the property's value intact.

How to use them

How to write price reduced posts that get enquiries

  1. Message everyone who enquired before the drop first, before you post publicly; they already like the property.
  2. Show the old and new prices side by side. The saving is the story.
  3. If the seller agrees, add a deadline; it gives fence-sitters a reason to move now.
  4. Don't over-explain why the price dropped. "Seller motivated" is enough.
  5. Refresh the cover photo so the listing looks new in feeds.

FAQ

Questions agents ask

How do I announce a price reduction without hurting the listing?

Call it a new price or an opportunity, keep the property's best features front and centre, and send it first to people who already showed interest.

Should I show the old price?

Yes, if the seller is comfortable. The visible saving creates urgency.

How long should a reduced price be valid?

Many agents use 1–3 weeks. A deadline only works if the seller will actually stick to it.

Is 'price reduced' good for rentals too?

Yes. Landlords often review rent after a few weeks vacant; post the new annual rent and any service charge.

Real estate captions

Open house & inspection captions for real estate

An open house or inspection day lives or dies on attendance. Your caption needs the when, the where and a reason to come this weekend instead of next.

In Lagos and Abuja most viewings are booked as inspections, not walk-in open houses, so give an RSVP route (WhatsApp is best) and a clear time window.

Always state the day, the time window and the general location. Share the exact address privately with people who RSVP; it's safer for you and the seller.

How to use them

How to write open house & inspection posts that get enquiries

  1. Post the invite 5–7 days ahead, a reminder the day before, and a live story on the day.
  2. Ask people to RSVP on WhatsApp so you collect numbers for follow-up even if they don't buy this one.
  3. Share the exact address only with confirmed attendees, and tell the estate gate who is coming.
  4. For land, a free group site inspection with a pick-up point gets far more turnout than "visit anytime".
  5. Within 24 hours, send every attendee a thank-you message with the price, documents and next step.

FAQ

Questions agents ask

What's the difference between an open house and an inspection?

An open house is a set window where anyone can walk through. An inspection is a booked viewing for one buyer or a small group. In Nigeria, booked inspections are more common.

When should I post an open house caption?

About a week before, again the day before, and live on the day through stories or WhatsApp status.

Should I post the full address?

It's safer to post the area and share the exact address after people RSVP.

Do people charge inspection fees?

Some agents in Nigeria do. If you charge one, state it clearly in the caption so there are no surprises at the gate.

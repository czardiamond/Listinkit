Off-plan captions for real estate

Off-plan buyers are buying a promise. Your caption needs the payment plan, the completion timeline and proof the developer can deliver.

Off-plan is big in Lagos, Abuja and Dubai, and the same rules apply: be specific about the payment plan, be honest about the completion date, and point to the developer's track record.

In Dubai, property ads must carry the advertising permit number required by the regulator; check the current rules with your brokerage before posting.

How to use them

How to write off-plan posts that get enquiries

  1. Always show the payment plan as a simple breakdown: deposit, instalments, tenure, and what happens if a payment is late.
  2. Never promise returns. "Potential value gain" with "not guaranteed" protects you and your client.
  3. Post real construction progress monthly; it is the strongest trust signal for off-plan buyers.
  4. Give the completion date as a quarter and year, and update it honestly if it moves.
  5. In Dubai, include the advertising permit number your regulator requires on every listing ad.

FAQ

Questions agents ask

What is an off-plan property caption?

A post that markets a property before or during construction, focusing on the payment plan, completion date, developer and early pricing.

How do I build trust in off-plan posts?

Name the developer and past projects, share real site progress, and make documents and contracts available for review.

Can I mention ROI on off-plan property?

Talk about potential, not guarantees. Avoid stating a fixed return unless it's contractually guaranteed by the developer.

Does this work for Dubai off-plan?

Yes, with AED pricing and the advertising permit number required for property ads in Dubai.

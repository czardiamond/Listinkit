Real estate captions

Sold captions for real estate

A sold post is the cheapest advert you'll ever run. It tells future sellers you close and tells buyers that good homes go fast.

The best sold captions aren't about you; they're about the client's win and what you did to get it. Mention the timeline, the challenge you solved, or how many viewings it took.

Always ask the client before tagging them or showing their face, and never post the final sale price without the seller's permission.

How to use them

How to write sold posts that get enquiries

  1. Shoot a 10-second handover clip; sold videos outperform static "SOLD" graphics on TikTok and reels.
  2. Turn every sold post into a lead magnet with one line: "I have buyers waiting for [Area] homes."
  3. Get written permission before tagging clients or showing faces, and keep the sale price private unless the seller agrees.
  4. Pin your three best sold posts to your Instagram grid as proof for new sellers.
  5. Send the sold post to the buyers who lost out, with a line that you're sourcing similar homes.

FAQ

Questions agents ask

Should I post the sale price?

Only with the seller's permission. "Sold above asking" or "sold in 9 days" gives social proof without disclosing the price.

What do I write in a sold caption?

Congratulate the client, give one result (speed, price, difficulty overcome) and add a call to action for new sellers or buyers.

Can I post a sold caption for a rental?

Yes. Use "LET" or "RENTED" and focus on how quickly you found a good tenant; landlords respond to that.

How soon after closing should I post?

Within a day or two while it still feels like news, and after documents are signed so nothing can fall through.

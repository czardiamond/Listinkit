Real estate captions

Just listed captions for real estate

The first 24 hours of a new listing get the most attention. Lead with the one feature a buyer would screenshot, then make booking an inspection effortless.

A good just listed caption does three jobs: it stops the scroll with a hook, it gives the three facts buyers filter on (type, location, price), and it tells people exactly how to book a viewing. Everything else is decoration.

Swap the bracketed parts for your listing. Keep the price visible if you can; in Nigerian and Gulf markets especially, "price on request" posts get far fewer serious DMs.

How to use them

How to write just listed posts that get enquiries

  1. Post the cover photo that shows the best room in daylight. On a phone the first image is the whole decision.
  2. Put type, area and price in the first two lines; Instagram cuts captions after roughly 125 characters.
  3. Use one call to action, not three. "DM VIEW" or "WhatsApp [number]" — pick the one you'll actually answer fastest.
  4. Repost to WhatsApp status within the hour; your contacts are warmer leads than Instagram strangers.
  5. Add the title document. In Nigeria, "C of O" or "Governor's Consent" in the caption filters out time-wasters early.

FAQ

Questions agents ask

What should a just listed caption include?

The property type, the area, the price (or price range), one standout feature and a single clear call to action such as a WhatsApp number or "DM VIEW".

How many hashtags should I use on a just listed post?

On Instagram, 5 to 15 relevant hashtags mixing city, area and property type works well. On TikTok, 3 to 5 is enough.

Should I put the price in the caption?

Usually yes. Visible prices attract buyers who can afford the property and reduce "how much?" DMs. If the seller insists on privacy, give a range.

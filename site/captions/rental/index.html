Real estate captions

Rental (to let) captions for real estate

Renters filter by three things: rent, location and what's included. Make the total cost clear and you'll spend less time on inspections that go nowhere.

In Lagos and Abuja, tenants usually pay rent yearly and also ask about service charge, agency and legal fees, and caution deposits. Listing these up front saves everyone time.

For London, Nairobi or Dubai rentals, switch to the local norms (monthly rent, deposit, Ejari in Dubai).

How to use them

How to write rental (to let) posts that get enquiries

  1. List every fee clearly (rent, service charge, agency, legal, caution). It builds trust and filters enquiries.
  2. Mention utilities tenants care about: prepaid meter, water, and power backup.
  3. Say who it suits ("ideal for a young family") to help the right people self-select.
  4. Collect budget and move-in date on WhatsApp before booking an inspection.
  5. For international rentals, follow local rules on fees and deposits before posting.

FAQ

Questions agents ask

What should a to-let caption include?

The rent (and whether it's yearly or monthly), location, bedrooms, service charge and fees, key utilities and how to book an inspection.

Should I include agency and legal fees?

Yes. Hidden fees are the biggest complaint tenants have; showing them builds credibility.

How do I write a caption for a self-contain or mini flat?

Highlight finishing (tiles, POP, cabinets), the compound (gated, quiet) and the annual rent clearly.

Do these work outside Nigeria?

Yes; use the monthly-rent example and adapt fees and deposits to local rules.

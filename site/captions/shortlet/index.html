Real estate captions

Shortlet captions for real estate

Shortlet guests book on feeling and amenities: power, Wi-Fi, security, the view and how fast you reply. Your caption should sell the stay, not the square metres.

Shortlets in Lagos (Lekki, VI, Ikoyi) and Abuja compete on reliability. "24/7 power" and "fast Wi-Fi" are selling points guests actively look for, so say them clearly if they are true.

State your house rules (caution deposit, parties, check-in times) up front to avoid awkward conversations later.

How to use them

How to write shortlet posts that get enquiries

  1. Only claim what is true every night: "24/7 power" means generator or inverter backup that actually works.
  2. Post your best photo at golden hour or at night with warm lights; shortlet guests buy the mood.
  3. Put the caution deposit and house rules in the caption or pinned story.
  4. Reply within minutes. In shortlets, the fastest reply usually wins the booking.
  5. Post availability on WhatsApp status every Thursday for weekend bookings.

FAQ

Questions agents ask

What should a shortlet caption include?

The area, number of bedrooms, nightly rate, key amenities (power, Wi-Fi, security, pool), house rules and how to book.

Should I post my nightly rate?

Yes, or a "from" rate. Guests compare quickly and skip posts without prices.

What hashtags work for shortlets in Lagos?

Mix area tags like #LekkiShortlet with general ones like #LagosShortlet and #Staycation. See the Lagos hashtag page for sets.

Can I use these for Airbnb listings?

Yes. Use the Instagram versions as social posts and the description templates for the Airbnb listing itself.

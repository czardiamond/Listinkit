Real estate captions

Luxury captions for real estate

Luxury copy whispers. Fewer emojis, more specifics: materials, views, privacy and the life the home makes possible.

High-net-worth buyers skim fast and hate hype. Replace "amazing" and "stunning" with concrete details: the ceiling height, the finishes, the smart-home system, the view.

Discretion is part of the product. Many luxury sellers prefer "price on application" and private viewings; respect that in the caption.

How to use them

How to write luxury posts that get enquiries

  1. Cut emojis to one or two, or none. Let the photography carry the excitement.
  2. Lead with one concrete luxury detail rather than adjectives.
  3. Offer private viewings and mention NDAs for off-market homes; it signals you handle discretion.
  4. Hire proper photography and video; luxury buyers judge the agent by the media.
  5. If the seller accepts foreign currency, say "USD offers considered" rather than listing two prices.

FAQ

Questions agents ask

How is a luxury caption different?

Shorter, calmer and more specific: materials, design, views and privacy, with fewer emojis and no hype words.

Should I show the price on luxury listings?

It depends on the seller. "Price on application" is common at the top end, though visible prices still filter enquiries.

What hashtags suit luxury listings?

Use a few premium-intent tags like #LuxuryRealEstate alongside the area tag, for example #IkoyiHomes or #DubaiMarina.

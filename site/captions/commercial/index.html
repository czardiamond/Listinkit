Real estate captions

Commercial property captions for real estate

Business tenants and investors care about numbers and logistics: footfall, frontage, power, parking, access and lease terms.

Commercial captions should read like a quick business case. Who is the space good for, and what makes it work for them? A pharmacy cares about footfall; a logistics firm cares about truck access.

Give the size in square metres, the lease terms (yearly, multi-year) and the service charge. Investors will also want the current tenancy and income if it's tenanted.

How to use them

How to write commercial property posts that get enquiries

  1. Lead with the business use: who the space suits and why.
  2. Always state size, rent per sqm or per year, and service charge.
  3. Mention power arrangements and parking; they are deal-breakers for Nigerian businesses.
  4. For investment sales, offer the tenancy schedule and rent roll under NDA.
  5. Post on LinkedIn as well as Instagram; commercial decision-makers are there.

FAQ

Questions agents ask

What makes a good commercial property caption?

A clear business case: size, location, access, power, parking, lease terms and who the space suits.

Should I post rent per square metre?

For offices, yes; it's how businesses compare. For shops, annual rent is usually clearer.

Is Instagram good for commercial property?

It helps for shops and small offices. For larger offices and investment sales, add LinkedIn and direct WhatsApp outreach.

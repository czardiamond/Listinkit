Land for sale captions for real estate

Land buyers ask the same four questions every time: where exactly, how big, what title, and can I pay in instalments. Answer them in the caption.

Land is the most searched category on Nigerian property boards and the one with the most fraud fears. Captions that state the title document and invite verification build trust fast.

Use the plot size your buyers use (in Lagos many people say "a plot"; always add the square metres so there's no confusion).

How to use them

How to write land for sale posts that get enquiries

  1. State the title document in every caption and offer documents for verification before payment.
  2. Give the size in square metres, not just "1 plot"; plot sizes differ between states and estates.
  3. Name the nearest major road or junction so buyers can judge access, without overstating it.
  4. Drone footage and a simple layout plan get more saves than a photo of bush.
  5. Make the payment plan explicit: deposit, tenure and whether prices rise during instalments.

FAQ

Questions agents ask

What should a land for sale caption include?

Location, size in square metres, title document, price per plot, access road, and payment plan if any.

Which title documents should I mention for land in Nigeria?

Mention exactly what the land has, such as Certificate of Occupancy, Governor's Consent, registered survey, gazette or excision. Never claim a title the land doesn't have.

How do I make land posts look trustworthy?

Show real site photos or video, invite buyers to verify documents with their lawyer, and run scheduled site inspections.

Is TikTok good for land sales?

Yes. Short plot walk-throughs and "what to check before you buy land" videos do well and build trust.

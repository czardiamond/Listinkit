WhatsApp templates

WhatsApp new listing messages for real estate

A new-listing broadcast should be readable in five seconds on a lock screen and end with one easy reply.

WhatsApp is where most Nigerian and many African property deals actually happen. A broadcast lands privately in each contact's chat, so it feels personal, but only if you write it that way.

Keep it under about 80 words, put the photo or video first, and ask for a one-word reply. One-word replies ("YES", "VIEW") massively lower the effort to respond.

How to use them

How to send them so people reply

  1. Personalise the first line with the contact's name; it looks like a private message, not spam.
  2. Use *bold* (asterisks) for the headline and price; WhatsApp formats it automatically.
  3. Send media first, then the text, so the caption sits right under the video.
  4. Segment your lists: buyers by budget, investors, diaspora, renters, and other agents.
  5. Send in the morning or early evening, not late at night, and never more than a few broadcasts a week.

FAQ

Questions agents ask

What's the difference between a WhatsApp broadcast and a group?

A broadcast list sends your message privately to each contact, and replies come back to you one-to-one. A group shows everyone's replies to everyone. Broadcasts only reach people who have saved your number, so ask clients to save it.

How many people can I add to a broadcast list?

WhatsApp limits list size (historically 256 contacts per list) and the limit can change, so split large audiences into several segmented lists.

How often should I send listing broadcasts?

A few times a week at most. Too many and contacts mute or block you, which hurts every future broadcast.

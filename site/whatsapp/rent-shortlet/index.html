How to use them

How to send them so people reply

  1. Save these as WhatsApp Business quick replies so you can answer in seconds.
  2. Collect ID for every shortlet guest and register them with estate security.
  3. State the caution deposit and refund timeline before payment.
  4. For long-term lets, list every fee in the first message so only qualified tenants book inspections.
  5. Ask for a review on the day of check-out while the stay is fresh.

FAQ

Questions agents ask

What's the difference between a WhatsApp broadcast and a group?

A broadcast list sends your message privately to each contact, and replies come back to you one-to-one. A group shows everyone's replies to everyone. Broadcasts only reach people who have saved your number, so ask clients to save it.

What should a shortlet confirmation message include?

Dates, check-in and check-out times, caution deposit, ID requirement and when the location pin will be sent.

How do I handle caution deposits?

State the amount and refund timeline before booking, inspect at check-out, and refund promptly with a short note on any deductions.

Can these work on Airbnb messaging?

Yes. The confirmation, check-in and check-out messages adapt well to Airbnb and Booking.com inboxes.

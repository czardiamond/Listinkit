WhatsApp follow-up messages for real estate

Most deals come from the second, third or fourth message, not the first. These follow-ups stay helpful instead of pushy.

Every follow-up should give the lead something: a new option, an answer, a document, or a deadline. "Just checking in" on its own rarely gets a reply.

Space them out (day 1, day 3, day 7, then monthly) and stop when someone asks you to.

How to use them

How to send them so people reply

  1. Reply to new enquiries within minutes; speed is the biggest single factor in converting WhatsApp leads.
  2. Every follow-up should add value: a video, a new option, an answer or a document.
  3. Use the 1-3-7 rhythm, then monthly. Stop if someone asks you to.
  4. Label chats (WhatsApp Business labels) as New, Viewing, Offer, Closed to see who needs a nudge.
  5. Send weekly updates to sellers even when there's no news; silence makes sellers switch agents.

FAQ

Questions agents ask

What's the difference between a WhatsApp broadcast and a group?

A broadcast list sends your message privately to each contact, and replies come back to you one-to-one. A group shows everyone's replies to everyone. Broadcasts only reach people who have saved your number, so ask clients to save it.

How many times should I follow up a property lead?

Three times in the first week (day 1, 3 and 7) and then about once a month, as long as each message adds something useful.

How do I follow up without being annoying?

Give a reason with every message, such as a new listing, an answer or a document, and offer an easy way to pause.

Should I use WhatsApp Business?

Yes, if you can. Labels, quick replies and a business profile make follow-ups faster and look more professional.

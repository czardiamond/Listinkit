How to use them

How to send them so people reply

  1. Plan the sequence: teaser, launch, payment-plan explainer, deadline, then monthly progress updates.
  2. Send the brochure as a PDF and the price list as an image; both are easy to forward.
  3. Never promise guaranteed returns or a completion date the developer won't put in the contract.
  4. Brief your realtor network separately with commission terms and a ready marketing pack.
  5. For Dubai, include the property advertising permit number your regulator requires.

FAQ

Questions agents ask

What's the difference between a WhatsApp broadcast and a group?

A broadcast list sends your message privately to each contact, and replies come back to you one-to-one. A group shows everyone's replies to everyone. Broadcasts only reach people who have saved your number, so ask clients to save it.

What should an off-plan WhatsApp message include?

The development, location, unit types, starting price, deposit, payment plan, completion date and a call to action for the brochure.

How do I handle 'what if the project is delayed?'

Point to the developer's track record and the contract's delay clause, and offer the contract for their lawyer to review.

How often should I send construction updates?

Monthly is enough to build trust without overwhelming buyers.

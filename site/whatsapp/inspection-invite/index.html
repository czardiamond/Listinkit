How to use them

How to send them so people reply

  1. Offer two or three specific slots; "when are you free?" leads to endless back-and-forth.
  2. Send the exact address and location pin only after confirmation.
  3. Remind people the day before and on the morning of the inspection.
  4. State any inspection fee before they travel, never at the gate.
  5. Follow up within 24 hours, whether they came or not.

FAQ

Questions agents ask

What's the difference between a WhatsApp broadcast and a group?

A broadcast list sends your message privately to each contact, and replies come back to you one-to-one. A group shows everyone's replies to everyone. Broadcasts only reach people who have saved your number, so ask clients to save it.

How do I reduce inspection no-shows?

Confirm in writing, send a reminder the day before, and send a location pin on the morning of the inspection.

Should I charge an inspection fee?

That's your call and common in some Nigerian markets. If you do, state it clearly before the inspection is booked.

Can I do inspections over WhatsApp video?

Yes. Live video inspections work well for diaspora and busy buyers, followed by an in-person visit before payment.

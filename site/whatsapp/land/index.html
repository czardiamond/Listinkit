How to use them

How to send them so people reply

  1. Never claim a title the land doesn't have. State exactly what exists: C of O, Governor's Consent, gazette, registered survey or excision.
  2. Invite verification by the buyer's lawyer; it's the fastest way to build trust.
  3. Tell buyers to pay only into the company or verified account, never a personal account you can't explain.
  4. Run fixed inspection days with a pick-up point; group inspections convert better than "come anytime".

FAQ

Questions agents ask

What's the difference between a WhatsApp broadcast and a group?

A broadcast list sends your message privately to each contact, and replies come back to you one-to-one. A group shows everyone's replies to everyone. Broadcasts only reach people who have saved your number, so ask clients to save it.

What should a WhatsApp land broadcast include?

Location, size in square metres, title document, price per plot, payment plan and the next inspection date.

How do I answer 'is the land genuine?'

Offer the documents and survey plan for their lawyer to verify, invite them to a site inspection, and give the company account for payment.

Should I send land prices in naira or dollars?

Price in naira for Nigerian land and give an approximate dollar figure for diaspora buyers, noting the exchange rate date.

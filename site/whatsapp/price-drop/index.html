How to use them

How to send them so people reply

  1. Re-contact previous enquirers individually before posting the drop publicly.
  2. Use strike-through (~text~) for the old price; WhatsApp formats it automatically.
  3. Keep a deadline honest; if it passes and the price stays, your next deadline won't be believed.
  4. Don't explain why the seller reduced the price; "motivated seller" is enough.
  5. Track who replies so you can prioritise viewings.

FAQ

Questions agents ask

What's the difference between a WhatsApp broadcast and a group?

A broadcast list sends your message privately to each contact, and replies come back to you one-to-one. A group shows everyone's replies to everyone. Broadcasts only reach people who have saved your number, so ask clients to save it.

How do I tell clients a price has dropped?

Send a short personal message to past enquirers first with the new price, then a broadcast to your wider list.

Can I show the old price?

Yes, with the seller's consent. Use WhatsApp strike-through formatting for the old figure.

Will a price drop make buyers think something's wrong?

Not if you frame it as a review and keep the property's strengths up front. Avoid desperate language.

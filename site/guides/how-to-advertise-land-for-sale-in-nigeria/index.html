How to advertise land for sale in Nigeria

Land sells on trust. The agents who move plots fastest make verification easy, show real proof, and post the same clear facts everywhere buyers look.

Land is one of the most-posted categories on Nigerian property boards; the Nairaland Properties board alone runs to thousands of pages of adverts. That also means buyers are wary. Your ad has to stand out by being clearer and more verifiable than the next one.

Below is a seven-step process, then a set of ad templates for each channel. Prices are blank on purpose; use the seller's real figure.

The 7-step process

1. Get the facts straight before you post. Confirm the exact size in square metres, the title document (C of O, Governor's Consent, registered survey, gazette or excision), whether the land is free of government acquisition, and who has authority to sell. Ask the seller for copies you can show a buyer's lawyer.

2. Shoot proof, not just bush. Walk the access road on video, film beacons if they're in place, take a drone or wide shot if you can, and photograph nearby landmarks so buyers can place it. Blur personal details on any document you show.

3. Write one master ad, then adapt it. Location, size, title, access, price per plot, payment plan, inspection day, contact. Every channel version below is a cut of that same fact sheet.

4. Post where Nigerian land buyers already look. The Nairaland Properties board, Jiji, Nigeria Property Centre and PropertyPro listings, Facebook property groups, your WhatsApp status and broadcast lists, Instagram and TikTok. Realtor networks run by developers are the biggest multiplier if you're marketing an estate.

5. Run fixed site inspections. A free group inspection every Saturday with a pick-up point converts far better than "come anytime". Collect WhatsApp numbers from everyone who registers.

6. Make verification easy. Invite buyers to verify documents with their own lawyer, and only accept payment into a company or clearly identified account. Transparency is your best sales tool in a market full of land scams.

7. Follow up within 24 hours. Send every enquirer and inspection attendee the layout, price, payment plan and a deadline if one genuinely exists.

How to use them

Tips that get more enquiries

  1. Never claim a title the land doesn't have. "Good title" on its own is a red flag to buyers.
  2. Always give size in square metres; "one plot" means different sizes in different places.
  3. Put the same core facts in every channel so buyers who see you twice see the same story.
  4. Use real video of the access road and plot; stock images destroy trust.
  5. Track which channel each enquiry came from so you can double down on what works.

FAQ

Questions agents ask

Where can I advertise land for sale in Nigeria for free?

The Nairaland Properties board, Jiji, Facebook property groups, your WhatsApp status and broadcast lists, Instagram and TikTok are all free. Property portals such as Nigeria Property Centre and PropertyPro also offer listings.

What documents should I mention in a land advert?

The exact title document, such as C of O, Governor's Consent, registered survey, gazette or excision, and whether a survey plan and deed of assignment are available.

How do I get buyers to trust my land ad?

Show real video, state the title precisely, invite verification by the buyer's lawyer, and run scheduled site inspections.

Should I include the price in a land ad?

Yes. Ads with a clear price per plot and payment plan attract more serious enquiries than "call for price".

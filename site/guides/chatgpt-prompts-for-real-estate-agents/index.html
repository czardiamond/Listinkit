ChatGPT prompts for real estate agents

ChatGPT writes better listings when you give it a role, the facts, the audience and the format. These prompts do that for you; fill the brackets and paste.

Generic prompts give generic copy. The prompts below always include four things: who ChatGPT should act as, the property facts, who the copy is for, and exactly what format you want back.

Always check the output. AI tools can invent features, distances or legal claims; never post a fact you haven't confirmed. Or skip the prompting entirely and use the free ListingKit generator, which is set up for these formats already.

Copy and adapt

Ready-to-copy templates

Listing copy

Portal descriptionLong form

Act as an experienced real estate copywriter in [City]. Write a listing description for [property type] in [area] with these facts only: [bedrooms, bathrooms, size, features, title document, price]. Audience: [families / investors / expats]. Format: a headline under 70 characters, a 120–160 word description in plain English, then a bullet list of features. Do not invent any facts, distances or amenities.

Short classifiedJiji / Craigslist style

Rewrite this listing as a classified ad under 50 words, keeping price, location, size and contact: [paste listing].

Five headlinesA/B testing

Give me 5 listing headlines under 60 characters for [property] in [area]. Make one feature-led, one price-led, one lifestyle-led, one investor-led and one urgency-led. No clickbait or claims I haven't given you.

Flyer copyA4 or square

Write flyer copy for [property]: one headline of up to 6 words, 4 bullets of up to 6 words each, the price [₦___] and a call to action with [phone]. Facts: [paste].

Social and WhatsApp

Instagram caption set3 tones

Write 3 Instagram captions for this listing: [facts]. Tone 1: friendly with emojis. Tone 2: luxury and understated. Tone 3: investor-focused. Each under 120 words, with a clear call to action to WhatsApp [number] and 8 relevant hashtags for [City/area].

WhatsApp broadcastShort and scannable

Write a WhatsApp broadcast message for [property] for my buyer list. Under 70 words, use WhatsApp formatting (*bold* for headline and price), 3 tick-mark bullets, and end by asking them to reply with one word to see the video.

TikTok hooksFirst 3 seconds

Give me 10 TikTok hooks (under 12 words each) for a house tour of [property] in [area] priced at [price]. Mix curiosity, POV, question and myth-busting styles.

30-second video scriptShot list

Write a 30-second property tour script for [property] filmed on a phone. Break it into timed shots (0–3s, 3–10s etc.) with what to film and what I say on camera. End with price and WhatsApp [number].

Leads and clients

Follow-up sequenceDay 1 / 3 / 7

Write a 3-message WhatsApp follow-up sequence for a buyer who enquired about [property] but hasn't booked a viewing. Day 1, day 3, day 7. Each message must add something new (video, similar option, question) and be under 50 words. Polite, never pushy.

Objection replyPrice too high

A buyer says [property] at [price] is too expensive. Write 3 short, respectful WhatsApp replies that explain the value using these facts [paste], offer an alternative, and keep the conversation open.

Seller updateWeekly report

Write a weekly update message to a seller. Data: [viewings], [enquiries], [feedback]. Be honest, suggest one next step, keep it under 100 words.

Landlord pitchWin a mandate

Write a short message to a landlord in [area] explaining why they should give me their [property type] to let. Mention [your track record], how I'll market it (Instagram, WhatsApp, portals) and my fee [__%]. Under 120 words.

Neighbourhood guideContent post

Write a 200-word neighbourhood guide to [area] for people relocating there. Only use these facts: [paste facts you've verified]. Sections: who it suits, getting around, everyday conveniences, what to ask an agent.

Repurpose one listingWhole kit

From this listing [paste], produce: 1) an Instagram caption, 2) a TikTok caption, 3) a WhatsApp broadcast, 4) flyer headline + 4 bullets, 5) a 30-second video script. Use only the facts given.

How to use them

Tips that get more enquiries

  1. Give ChatGPT a role, the facts, the audience and the output format every time.
  2. Add "do not invent facts" — AI tools happily make up pools, distances and title documents.
  3. Paste your best past caption and ask it to match the style.
  4. Ask for several options and pick, rather than accepting the first answer.
  5. Never paste a client's private documents or personal data into an AI tool.

FAQ

Questions agents ask

What is the best ChatGPT prompt for a property listing?

One that sets a role, gives only verified facts, names the audience and specifies the format and length, plus an instruction not to invent details.

Can ChatGPT write WhatsApp broadcasts for real estate?

Yes, if you specify length, WhatsApp formatting and a single call to action. Check every fact before sending.

Is it okay to post AI-written listings?

Yes, as long as every fact is accurate and the copy follows local advertising rules. You're responsible for what you post.

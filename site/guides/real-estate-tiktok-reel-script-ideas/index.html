Real estate TikTok and reel script ideas

Twelve short-video formats that work for agents, each with a timed shot list you can film on a phone in under ten minutes.

Short video is where new buyers discover agents now. You don't need a crew: a phone, natural light, a steady hand and a script that hooks in the first three seconds.

Each script below runs 15–45 seconds. Film vertically (9:16), keep text on screen short, and always end with one clear call to action.

Copy and adapt

Ready-to-copy templates

1. The 30-second house tourClassic

[0–3s] Door opens: "Come see a [4]-bed in [area] for [price]."
[3–10s] Living room sweep.
[10–17s] Kitchen detail.
[17–25s] Master suite + view.
[25–30s] To camera: "WhatsApp [number] to view."

2. POV: you just got the keysEmotional

[0–3s] Keys in hand at the gate. Text: "POV: you just bought in [area]".
[3–15s] Walk through slowly, music only.
[15–20s] Balcony view.
[20–25s] Text: "Could be you. [price]".

3. What ₦___ gets you in 3 areasComparison

[0–3s] "What ₦___ buys you in [area A], [B] and [C]."
[3–12s] Property A highlights.
[12–21s] Property B.
[21–30s] Property C.
[30–35s] "Which would you pick? Comment."

4. Myth-bustingAuthority

[0–3s] "'You need ₦___ cash to buy land' — false."
[3–20s] Explain payment plans with on-screen numbers.
[20–25s] "Here's one in [area]."
[25–30s] CTA.

5. 3 red flags before you buy landEducation

[0–3s] "3 red flags when buying land in [City]."
[3–10s] 1: Seller can't show title.
[10–17s] 2: No survey plan.
[17–24s] 3: Pressure to pay into a personal account.
[24–30s] "Save this. Follow for more."

6. Price revealEngagement

[0–3s] "Guess the price of this [area] apartment."
[3–20s] Quick tour.
[20–25s] Pause: "Comment your guess."
[25–30s] Reveal price on screen.

7. Day in the life of a realtorPersonal brand

[0–5s] Morning coffee + phone full of enquiries.
[5–15s] Inspection in [area].
[15–25s] Signing / handover moment (with consent).
[25–30s] "Want me to find yours? Link in bio."

8. Neighbourhood in 30 secondsArea guide

[0–3s] "Thinking of moving to [area]? 30 seconds."
[3–10s] Getting around.
[10–17s] Everyday conveniences.
[17–24s] Who it suits.
[24–30s] "Homes here from [price]. DM me."

9. Before & afterRenovation / construction

[0–3s] Split screen: shell vs finished.
[3–20s] Room-by-room transitions.
[20–30s] "Off-plan from [price]. Completion [date]."

10. Client storySocial proof

[0–3s] "[Client] was renting for 8 years. Today…"
[3–15s] Keys handover (with permission).
[15–25s] Client says one line on camera.
[25–30s] "Your turn? WhatsApp [number]."

11. Shortlet night tourMood

[0–3s] Door opens to warm lights: "Your weekend in [area]."
[3–15s] Lounge, Netflix, bedroom.
[15–22s] Pool or balcony at night.
[22–28s] "From [price]/night. Book on WhatsApp."

12. Answer a commentCommunity

[0–3s] Screenshot of a comment: "How much is rent in [area]?"
[3–25s] Answer with 2–3 real examples you have.
[25–30s] "Drop your next question below."

How to use them

Tips that get more enquiries

  1. Hook in the first 3 seconds with a number, a question or a POV.
  2. Film in daylight and walk slowly; shaky, fast footage loses viewers.
  3. Add on-screen text for people watching on mute.
  4. Post consistently (3–4 times a week) rather than one perfect video a month.
  5. Get written permission before filming clients or showing a seller's personal items.
  6. Reuse the same video on TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and WhatsApp status.

FAQ

Questions agents ask

How long should a real estate TikTok be?

15 to 45 seconds works for most tours and tips. Hook in the first 3 seconds.

What real estate videos get the most views?

Price reveals, POV tours, area comparisons, myth-busting and red-flag education tend to drive engagement and saves.

Do I need professional equipment?

No. A recent phone, natural light and a cheap stabiliser or steady hands are enough to start.
